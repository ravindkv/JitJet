
This is the constitution of the JitJet book which every chapter has to follow. Every chapter starts with the motivation connected to the previous chapter:
(1) First we show the animated video
(2) Then standalone script
(3) Then Theory
(4) Then validation from official packages
(5) Then Further reading

The sub-rules for "Theory": every sub-section of the theory has to follow this format:
(3a) first give the motivation
(3b) then draw few diagrams which could be some sktech or feynman diagrams using tikz to illustrate what we are going to explain
(3c) then describe the equation
(3d) Every equation has to be backed with numbers (which could be retrived from log files of standalone script) for a better understanding
(3e) Every equation has to be suplemented  by plots (which could be produced by standalone script or new scripts closely related to the standalone script) for a better understanding

The sub-rules for the "Further reading" section: it will have go in the following order:

(5a) Pitfalls and FAQs for newcomers
(5b) Exercises
(5c) Open problems in the context of Phase-2 LHC. The open problems has to be listed after thorough literature survey on the state-of-the-art understanding with proper citations.
