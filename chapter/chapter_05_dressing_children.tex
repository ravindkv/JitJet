\begin{journeybox}
Before the pilgrimage begins the children are dressed as hadrons. The mother's
share of the energy is carried by a B~hadron, which lives long enough to walk a
few millimetres on its own before it decays, and some of its decay products,
neutrinos and soft muons, leave the family without a trace.
\end{journeybox}
