Summary
-------
 * Chapter 9 -> 10: the hand-over propagates correctly. Every count and energy I traced agrees.
 * CMSSW: the pile-up is on the same footing (tracker hits per subdetector within 5 %, EB / EE /
   HB / HE energy per collision within 1-8 %). Not on the same footing: HF photoelectrons (+24 %),
   muon-chamber hits (x 1.6), number of HCAL cells hit, and three rows of the stage table whose
   definitions differ between the two sides.
 * Bugs: one [A] in geant4_SIM.py (the "boundary" of a primary is not the tracker boundary), with
   consequences for the stage table, the fate table and one sentence of the field section. A few
   [B] counting issues. Several text numbers no longer match the logs.

2.2 Not on the same footing
---------------------------
C1 [A] "Primaries leaving the tracker towards the calorimeters": 286 of 672 against 310 of 571.
       The two sides do not count the same thing. CMSSW counts every primary that crosses the
       tracker/calorimeter boundary, the forward ones included: 557 of 571 on straight lines.
       Ours puts the forward ones into a separate fate "beam pipe" (80 in the full run, 116 on
       straight lines), so it starts from 556 of 672. Like for like:
           straight lines   (556 + 116) / 672 = 100 %      CMSSW 557 / 571 = 97.5 %
           full physics     (286 +  80) / 672 = 54.5 %     CMSSW 310 / 571 = 54.3 %
       As printed, the "three ways" table and the stage table suggest 43 % against 54 %.
       See also bug B1: the split between "calorimeter" and "beam pipe" is itself not well defined.
       Fix: one definition (crossing of r = 123.3 cm or |z| = 293.5 cm, as CMSSW), then both rows agree.

C2 [B] Stage table, tracker hits: "from 6734 to 10 272" (CMSSW) is quoted next to our 3636 -> 12 623
       as if the columns matched ("Its stage table reads like ours"). They do not in the first three
       rows: CMSSW's 6734 contains 3283 crossings of neutral primaries (its own text says so, and the
       .md of our chapter subtracts them: (6734 - 3283) / 277 = 12.5). Ours counts charged only.
       Charged only, CMSSW goes 3451 -> 3736 -> ... -> 10 272.
       Fix: quote 3451, or say that the CMSSW transport rows include neutral crossings.

C3 [B] Stage table, the effect of decays. Ours: + 205 secondaries and + 2007 tracker hits
       (4443 -> 6450). CMSSW: + 66 secondaries and + 285 hits (7019 -> 7304). A factor 7 in hits.
       Part is definition (C4), but the hits are counted the same way on both sides once ionisation
       is off. Candidate reason: without energy loss a looper circles until it decays or until 500 ns,
       and its daughters loop again; the two sides may stop such tracks differently (CMSSW's
       SteppingAction has its own looper and step-number limits). Not understood; worth one test:
       geant4_SIM.py --stage B_Decay, hits by generation and by time.

C4 [B] Secondaries. Ours 4403 (704 / 2359 / 1231) for 672 primaries; CMSSW 1355 (196 / 773 / 386) for
       571: 6.6 against 2.4 per primary. And "tracks" in the hit ledger: 5.96 per primary (ours)
       against 3.09 (CMSSW SimTracks). The definitions differ: ours is "born in the tracker volume
       with more than 10 MeV" (neutrinos, pi0 photons and slow neutrons included), CMSSW's is "kept
       as a SimTrack" (its persistence rules). The text says "the secondaries grow to 1355" as if
       comparable. Fix: name the two definitions, or do not put the numbers side by side.

C5 [B] HF photoelectrons. Signal: 1671 (ours) against 1849. Pile-up per collision: 305 against 245,
       +24 % (+28 % per primary). The .md says the constants of the parametrisation "were chosen to
       give this scale", but they were chosen on one signal event in which a single pi+ makes 809 of
       CMSSW's 1849 photoelectrons. On 960 collisions the scale is a quarter too high.
       The stage trend is also opposite: ours falls 2063 -> 2117 -> 2123 -> 1926 -> 1671, CMSSW's
       rises 1511 -> 1533 -> 1607 -> 1599 -> 1849. Reason: ours answers to every particle that
       reaches the steel in every stage; in CMSSW the library answers to electromagnetic particles
       and the hadrons need the hadronic physics to give light.
       Fix: tune PE_HF on the crowd (target 245 per collision), and say in the stage table why the
       HF column falls.

C6 [B] Muon-chamber hits of the crowd: 1320 / 960 = 1.38 per collision against 838 / 995 = 0.84.
       By detector (ours / CMSSW): DT 209 / 70, CSC 856 / 710, RPC 35 / 9, GEM 220 / 49.
       Candidate reasons, both in our set-up: (a) one production cut per region, the electron's, also
       for photons (MuonChamber 0.002 mm instead of 1 mm, DT-CSC gas 1 mm instead of 25 mm, RPC gas
       8 mm instead of 8.5 m): many more soft photons are created in and around the gas;
       (b) the 2021 geometry. The chapter says the electron's cut is used "for both" but not that
       it costs a factor in the muon hits. The numbers are small and tail-driven (80 of the family's
       82 hits are one proton), so this is a caution, not a proof.

C7 [B] Cells hit. ECAL: ours 29 938 cells for 638 GeV (47 per GeV), CMSSW 12 804 for 362 GeV (35).
       HCAL, signal: HB 1281 cells for 0.83 GeV against 173 for 0.22; HE 1858 for 2.64 against 648
       for 1.66. HCAL, sum: HB 8632 of 9072 (95 %) against 6856 (76 %); HE 6147 against 5464;
       HO 166 against 66. Energies agree (2.1), cell counts do not: we have 2 to 4 times more cells
       per GeV in the HCAL. Likely the Russian roulette of CMSSW (fewer slow neutrons and soft
       photons, each with a weight: same energy, fewer cells), which the chapter lists as "not done"
       without saying it shows in every occupancy number ("95 %", "the crowd reaches ... 95 % of
       the endcap cells", "HO 8 %").

C8 [C] Rows of the "three ways" table that are compatible within statistics but look like trends:
       photons above 1 GeV converting 39 of 61 (64 +- 6 %) against 15 of 29 (52 +- 9 %); charged
       hadrons interacting 45 of 126 (36 +- 4 %) against 19 of 73 (26 +- 5 %). Our own gun gives
       26 % for 5 GeV pions; the 36 % is for p > 0.5 GeV, loopers included. A footnote would help.

3. BUGS AND WEAK POINTS IN example/SIM
====================================================================================================

B1 [A] geant4_SIM.py: the "boundary" of a primary is its last step in ANY recorded volume, not the
       crossing of the tracker boundary.  (RecSD.ProcessHits sets app.last1 on every step of track 1
       in the regions "in" AND "out"; App.end() lines 746-758 use the last one.)
       [checked] in simhits_signal.npz, of the 286 primaries with fate "calorimeter":
         - 71 have their "boundary" at |z| = 652-657 cm, r = 24-54 cm: the end of the beam-pipe
           volume, far behind the tracker (the region "in" contains beampipeBEAM);
         - 30 have it at r = 2.5 cm, |z| = 160-245 cm: where they left the beam-pipe volume, inside
           the tracker;
         - 89 of the 286 are primaries with |eta| > 3.
       Consequences:
       (a) "calorimeter" against "beam pipe" (r < 30 cm and |z| > 280 cm at the last step) is decided
           by where the beam-pipe volume happens to end. A looper of |eta| = 0.43 is "beam pipe"; a
           primary of |eta| = 4 that leaves the pipe at z = 215 cm is "calorimeter". One member of
           jet [0] (eta = -1.4) "left along the beam pipe" in the text: it left through the end of
           the tracker at small radius, towards the endcap, not along the pipe.
       (b) In the stages without hadronic physics a charged primary flies on into the muon system
           (5908 muon hits on straight lines), and the region "out" overwrites last1. The column
           "jet pT at bound." is then the momentum at the last step in the muon system.
           [checked] helix estimate for the 31 members at r = 123.3 / |z| = 293.5 cm in 3.8 T:
           94.4 GeV. The table says 91.2, and the text of the field section builds a sentence on it
           ("arrives at the calorimeters with 91.2 instead of 99.8 GeV"). CMSSW's b system moves
           from 56.8 to 56.6 in the same row. Rows 3 and 4 (81.5, 44.9) are affected too. The last
           row (42.5, 18 members) is safe: the members stop in the calorimeters.
       (c) prim_bnd, and with it r_b, z_b, pT_b of the primary listing in event_SIM.lhe, is not "at
           the tracker boundary" for a third of the rows that have one.
       (d) A primary stopped by the 500 ns cut in a recorded volume keeps its previous step, alive,
           and becomes "calorimeter" with a boundary inside the tracker. Rare in the full run.
       Fix: record the boundary once, at the first step of track 1 that ends outside
       r < R_TRK, |z| < Z_TRK; decide "beam pipe" there by |eta| of the crossing point or drop the
       category (C1). Then re-make the stage table and tab. fates.

B2 [B] geant4_SIM.py: neutrinos are treated as ordinary particles by the sensitive detectors.
       - HFSD: a neutrino that reaches the HF steel has a != 13, so it is stopped and its total
         energy is turned into photoelectrons (hadronic constants) and added to "at the HF".
       - DeadSD: its energy is added to the dead region.
       - births: 115 of the "704 secondaries from decays above 10 MeV" are neutrinos [checked:
         161 neutrino trajectories in g4tracks_signal.npz, 7 above 0.3 GeV, 13 GeV in all].
       The effect on the HF is small for the family (at most a few GeV of 4758) but it is light from
       a neutrino. Fix: skip |pid| in (12, 14, 16) in HFSD, DeadSD and in the secondary count
       (or kill neutrinos at birth, as CMSSW's StackingAction does: chapter 9 quotes exactly that).

B3 [B] geant4_SIM.py: the process counters count the end of a track as an occurrence.
       app.n[proc] += 1 runs "if ns or dead". For eIoni / hIoni / muIoni / ionIoni this adds every
       charged particle that ranges out in the tracker volume to the "delta rays". The chapter
       quotes 60 056 delta rays three times. [checked] only 7516 recorded tracks (above 0.2 MeV)
       were created by ionisation; the rest are softer delta rays from the sensors and the beam pipe
       plus the stops, in unknown proportion. Fix: count "ns > 0" for the ionisation processes, and
       stops separately.

B4 [B] geant4_SIM.py: HF energy of a hyperon. e = kinetic for p and n, kinetic + 2 m for antiprotons
       and antineutrons, TOTAL energy for everything else: a Lambda or Sigma arrives with its mass
       added (1.1 GeV), a proton without. Minor; make it "kinetic for baryons".

B5 [B] Cells from positions: larger than "a few millimetres" (tex caveat, .md, docstring).
       eb_codes / ee_codes use a grid that points to the origin. The barrel crystals are tilted by
       3 degrees and the endcap crystals point 1.3 m behind the collision point (2 to 8 degrees), so
       over the 22-23 cm of a crystal the grid drifts by 1.2 cm (EB) and up to 2 cm (EE): half a
       cell to a whole cell at the rear face, a few millimetres only at the shower maximum.
       Evidence that it matters: EE "cells in detector" is 17 376 (the grid of n_cells(), ring
       31.9 < r < 154.7 cm), the figure caption of the same section says 14 648 crystals (the real
       number, from the geometry), and the sum has 16 513 EE cells hit: more than exist.
       geant4_SIM.ee_codes also has no ring check, unlike standalone_SIM.ecal_codes.
       Affects: every EE cell count and occupancy ("95 % of the endcap cells"), "family keeps",
       and the crystal-sharing numbers if they are ever made in the endcap.
       Fix (best): take the crystal from the touchable (copy numbers), as CMSSW does; Geant4 knows
       it for free. Fix (minimal): correct the caveat and the 17 376.

B6 [B] One time per cell. A calorimeter row keeps the energy-weighted mean time of everything a
       primary (signal) or a collision (crowd) left in the cell during 500 ns. [checked] for the
       family, 11 % of the HB light, 6 % of the HE light and 1.2 % of the crystal energy sits in
       cells whose mean time is beyond 50 ns. A cell with a prompt shower and a neutron capture at
       300 ns gets one time in between and the full energy. CMSSW keeps the hits in time slices and
       the digitiser's window drops the late part. This is the main trap for chapter 11, and a
       likely part of the HCAL overshoot (B7). Fix: store the cell energy in a few time bins
       (e.g. 0-25, 25-50, 50-100, > 100 ns), or at least the prompt energy next to the total.

B7 [B] HCAL scale. guns_SIM.log: (crystals + 125 x scintillator) / E = 1.05 at 20 GeV, 1.10 at 50,
       1.11 at 100 GeV. The caveats say the factors are "not a calibration", but the text also says
       "the test beams of CMS measured the same shape": the shape perhaps, the level not (test beam:
       below 1 at all energies with this normalisation). An overshoot of 15-25 % at SIM level means
       every "HCAL x factor" number of the ledger (98 GeV, 32.1, the 302 GeV of the crowd) carries
       it. To check before chapter 11: late energy (B6), no Russian roulette, which volumes are
       "materialsScintillator" (34 HB volume types), and whether CMSSW weights layer 0.
       Also the statistics: 17 pions at 100 GeV, 27 at 50 GeV, and only those that reach the
       calorimeters as primaries (43 of 50 at 20 GeV); the text does not say so.

B8 [C] Tracker hit = entry point, time, energy loss, |p|, pid. No exit point, no direction, no path
       length. The digitiser of chapter 11 needs the segment inside the sensor for charge sharing
       (CMSSW's PSimHit has entry and exit). Cheap to add now, a re-simulation later.
       Related: with ionisation on, a step with zero deposit is not recorded, so its "ended at a
       boundary" flag is lost and two crossings could merge. [checked] no zero-energy hit in the
       signal file; 45 hits below 1 keV.

B9 [C] he_codes clips ieta to 17..29: the part of tower 16 that is read out in the endcap is
       booked in tower 17.

B10 [C] Energy closure is not shown. [checked] family: 7139.6 GeV at the gate, 5821.4 GeV in the
       tallies (tracker volume, muon system, crystals, preshower, scintillator, HF, dead region):
       81.5 %. The missing 1318 GeV is in volumes without a tally (brass, ECAL structure, coil,
       forward shielding) plus what escapes. A column "elsewhere" (one catch-all tally) would let
       the chapter show that nothing is lost, and would have caught B2.

B11 [C] Provenance. geant4_SIM.py was last written at 18:07 on 26 Nov, after all its outputs
       (crowd 16:54 / 17:43, stages 17:44-17:47, signal 17:52). The three JSON files agree on
       version, physics list, Birks, regions and sensitive volumes [checked], so the samples are
       consistent with each other; whether the script on disk is the one that made them cannot be
       told from the files.

B12 [C] add_hits_SIM.py: "ES cells in detector" is "--" (n_cells() has no ES); the real number is
       137 216 strips, and CMSSW quotes 82 999 of them. HF: 1728 cells here, 3456 in CMSSW's SIM
       numbering (dual read-out); say which.

       Per crossing the statement would be very different.

T12 [C] Intro: the forward calorimeter "is parametrised as in CMSSW". CMSSW uses a library of
       simulated showers; ours is a two-constant formula with a log-normal smearing. "Not showered,

T13 [C] Decay section: "147 neutrinos leave unseen" (plot_theory_SIM.json) against 161 neutrino

T14 [C] Guns: the 10 GeV photon numbers of the text (183 unconverted, 0.68 / 0.93; 117 converted,
